# Introduction to Gold Mining in Nova Scotia

Nova Scotia has a rich history of gold mining dating back to the 1860s. The province was once one of Canada's most productive gold mining regions.

## Discovery

The first significant gold discovery in Nova Scotia occurred in 1858 when a farmer named John Pulsifer found gold in Mooseland. This discovery sparked a gold rush that would transform the province.

## Key Points

- **First Discovery**: 1858 in Mooseland
- **Peak Production**: 1890s-1900s
- **Total Gold Produced**: Over 1 million ounces
- **Number of Districts**: More than 60 gold districts

## Impact on Nova Scotia

The gold rush brought:

1. Economic prosperity
2. Population growth
3. Infrastructure development
4. Technological innovation

> "The discovery of gold in Nova Scotia was a transformative event that shaped the province's economic and social landscape for generations."
